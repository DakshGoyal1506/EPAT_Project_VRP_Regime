# Known Limitations

1. Public data sources can change format, history depth, or availability without notice.
2. Yahoo Finance and NSE data feeds may be fragile and can require manual validation after refreshes.
3. The research pipeline is a proxy for executable risk management, not a claim of live trading readiness.
4. Backtest results are only as reliable as the alignment, lookahead, and calendar assumptions behind the inputs.
5. The broker layer is paper-signal oriented and must remain separate from any live-order workflow.
